# The Last Census

*The Domain is gone. Its paperwork isn't.*

On the first day of the campaign, an automated Domain office at the northern edge of the Persean Sector wakes on
schedule and resumes the **Grand Audit**: a census of every colony and a registry check of every vessel in the
Sector, overdue by two centuries. Its Assessor cutters fly out into a Sector that has never heard of them. They hail
captains, demand manifests, renew registrations that lapsed before the Collapse, and, now and then, seize a ship.

The tone is dry and a little melancholy, with machines that are very polite about a world that no longer exists.

## What it adds
- **The Assessment Bureau**, a new faction of Domain civil-service automata with its own station and system north of the core worlds.
- **Audits.** Bureau Assessor fleets roam the core worlds and will hail you. Comply, bluff, argue, or refuse; each choice has consequences.
- **A questline** that starts on its own in the first days of a new campaign: no console commands, no hidden prerequisites.
- **Four Bureau hulls**, from a census frigate to a treasury battleship, with original art in a consistent ivory-and-jade livery.
- **Five weapons** with distinct mechanics, including a beam that serves a *writ of seizure*: ships destroyed under a writ are always recoverable after the battle.
- **A built-in hullmod and two new ship systems** built around flux warfare and the Bureau's sense of accounting.
- **Secrets**, for captains who are curious, persistent, or simply honest.

## Requirements
- Starsector **0.98a**
- [LazyLib](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=5444.0)
- [LunaLib](https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=25658.0) (in-game settings menu)
- Optional: GraphicsLib. If it is enabled, Bureau projectiles and beams cast dynamic light.

## Installation
1. Extract the `TheLastCensus` folder into `Starsector/mods/`.
2. Enable **The Last Census**, **LazyLib** and **LunaLib** in the launcher.
3. Start a new game (recommended). The mod can also be added to an existing save: the Bureau's systems are generated the first time the save is loaded.

Removing the mod from a save that has used it is **not supported** (the faction, its systems and scripts are stored in the save).

## Settings (LunaLib menu: F3 by default, or Shift+F2)
- **Assessors audit your fleet**: turn audits of your fleet on or off. The main questline can still be completed with audits off.
- **Maximum Assessor circuits**: how many Assessor fleets the Bureau keeps in flight (0–6, default 3).

## Compatibility
Uses only its own ids (prefix `tlc_`) and does not modify any vanilla file or data. Built for the vanilla sector
layout; it has **not** been tested with Nexerelin's random-sector mode or with other mods that rewrite the core sector.
