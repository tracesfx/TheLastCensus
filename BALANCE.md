# Balance — The Last Census

Every number in the tables below was generated by a script from `data/hulls/ship_data.csv`,
`data/weapons/weapon_data.csv` and the vanilla 0.98a CSVs, so the tables match the data. Bureau content is in **bold**.
"Sustained DPS" = damage × burst ÷ (chargeup + chargedown + (burst − 1) × burst delay). Ammo-limited and burst weapons
are marked n/a, because a single number would mislead.

## Design intent
The Bureau's identity is **methodical attrition**. Its ships are slightly more flux-rich and slightly less armoured
than their vanilla peers. Their weapons are a little weaker on paper but get better the longer an engagement lasts:
Accrual stacks, Foreclosure hits harder against high flux, Surcharge raises enemy flux costs, Injunction cuts enemy
rate of fire. Their built-in hullmod pays them back for finishing kills. They should feel strong in sustained line
fights and weak in alpha-strike races, against fast flankers, and against carriers, since they bring no fighters at all.

### Hull rationale
| Hull | Price | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tally (5 DP) | Same as Wolf and Vigilance | Equal speed to the Wolf with a wider but less efficient shield (0.8 vs 0.7), 5 fewer OP, and a far weaker system (Maneuvering Jets vs Displacer). In exchange it has four turreted slots and 180° shields. It is a picket and Writ carrier, not a duelist. |
| Assessor (11 DP) | Between Hammerhead (10) and Medusa (12) | Less hull and armour than a Hammerhead, more flux and dissipation (5600/320 vs 4200/250), 5 more OP, and a narrower shield (240° vs 300°). Surcharge is a fleet-support debuff rather than a self-buff like Ammo Feeder, so the Assessor costs 1 DP more for a better fleet contribution. |
| Magistrate (22 DP) | Eagle (20) +2 | Same durability as the Eagle with slightly less dissipation (660 vs 700), plus a spinal large energy mount, a medium missile slot and Surcharge at cruiser range (1000 su). The large slot and the system justify the premium. It stays under the Champion (25), which has more armour and High Energy Focus. |
| Exchequer (45 DP) | Odyssey (45), between Onslaught (40) and Paragon (60) | A defensive battleship. It has the Paragon's Fortress Shield, but as a 240° front shield at 0.8 efficiency (Paragon: 360° omni at 0.6). Less hull than an Onslaught but much better flux (21000/1050 vs 17000/600). Three large slots, like the Onslaught; the Paragon has four. The 800 cargo is a real perk but no freighter replacement: 45 supplies/month against the Colossus's 6. |
| Plenipotentiary (36 DP) | Above Doom (35) | A unique, single-copy prize from a hidden boss fight. Deliberately the best cruiser in the mod (210 OP, 300° omni shield at 0.6), priced above Aurora (30) and Doom (35) so it is a heavy fleet investment rather than a free win. |

### Weapon rationale
| Weapon | Trade-off |
|---|---|
| Writ Projector (4 OP) | Strictly worse than a Tactical Laser as a weapon: two thirds the DPS, 60% of the range, same OP. You buy it for the seizure effect (a ship destroyed within 3 s of a Writ hit is always recoverable), which saves story points and skips the random "unrecoverable" roll. It does nothing for damage, so it never belongs in a slot that matters. |
| Subpoena SRM (4 OP) | Far less damage than a Harpoon (200 vs 750) at shorter range. In return it has four shots, good tracking and 700 EMP to knock out weapons and engines. It is a control tool, not a finisher. |
| Accrual Autocannon (11 OP) | At base it loses to the Heavy Autocannon on every axis (200 vs 214 DPS, 700 vs 800 range, 1.00 vs 0.90 flux per damage, +1 OP). After 1.25 s of uninterrupted hits it overtakes it (+25%: 250 DPS at 0.80), and it caps at +50% (300 DPS at 0.67) after 2.5 s. It rewards focus fire and punishes target switching and range games. |
| Injunction Emitter (10 OP) | Two thirds of a Pulse Laser's DPS at worse flux efficiency (1.10 vs 0.80). Every hit applies a non-stacking 4 s debuff (−20% enemy rate of fire and beam damage). It is a suppression weapon, not a damage dealer. |
| Foreclosure Battery (22 OP) | Well below the Plasma Cannon (467 vs 750 DPS, for 8 OP less) and around the HIL (467 vs 500, but energy against HE and hard flux on shields), with longer range than the Plasma Cannon. Against ships above 50% flux it deals +50% (≈700 DPS), finishing what Surcharge and Accrual start. |

## Hullmod and systems
| Effect | Numbers | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Assessor Protocols: Lien & Settlement | When an enemy dies, each surviving ship that damaged it sheds flux equal to 50% of the damage it dealt, capped at 30% of its own capacity | Pays out only on kills, scales with contribution, and is capped, so it cannot sustain a losing fight. Comparable in spirit to vanilla's kill-triggered effects. It is built in and hidden, so other hulls cannot buy it. |
| Surcharge (Assessor, Magistrate) | 6 s active, 20 s cooldown, 5% flux to use. Enemies within 800 su (1000 su for cruisers) pay +35% weapon flux and take +15% shield damage. Does not stack with itself. | A flux-war debuff: strong against energy boats and shield tanks, weak against shieldless armour tanks. The short window and long cooldown keep uptime around 23%. |
| Summary Judgment (Plenipotentiary only) | 8 s active, 24 s cooldown, 8% flux to use. One target within 1200 su cannot start a vent and takes +25% shield damage. | Single-target, needs a lock, and blocks only the *start* of a vent (one in progress finishes), so it punishes vent timing without hard-locking a ship. |

## Fleet strength
* **Assessor circuits (audits):** 30–60 FP at game start, +10 FP per in-game year after cycle 206 (capped at +40), quality 0.8, no officers. A starting fleet can usually beat a small circuit, so refusing an audit is a real choice with a real cost.
* **Relay salvagers (quest):** a pirate fleet sized to 80% of the player's fleet points, clamped to 50–180 FP.
* **Inspector-General (secret):** Plenipotentiary with a level-7 officer, plus 2 Magistrates, 2 Assessors and 2 Tallies (≈112 DP). A mid-game set-piece that the player chooses when to fight: "Proceed" on contact, or present yourself at the Tallyhouse later.

## Economy safety
* **Audits only ever cost the player.** The levy is 250 credits per ship (500–6000), plus 500/1000/2000 per gamma/beta/alpha core in cargo. Drugs and organs are confiscated with no compensation, so there is no buy-cheap, get-seized, redeem loop.
* **Census returns** pay 1000 × colony size (+1500 for each of the three quest returns), **once per colony** (market memory flag). With ~40 eligible colonies in a typical sector, the lifetime ceiling is roughly 200–250k credits, and earning it means visiting every colony in the Sector. That is comparable to a few mid-tier bounties and is not repeatable.
* **Quest rewards** are one-time: either the Bureau Charter Archive (blueprint package) plus a Magistrate, or an Exchequer plus 100,000 credits plus permanent audit immunity.
* **The Tallyhouse** is a size-4 station with standard vanilla industries (Population, Spaceport, Heavy Industry, Military Base, Battlestation, Heavy Batteries) and no black market. It joins the economy like any small market, and no new commodity is added, so vanilla prices are untouched.
* **Dead Letter Office cache** (secret, once): 2× Foreclosure Battery, 4× Writ Projector, 2× Accrual Autocannon.
